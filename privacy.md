# Privacy Policy — EPR Gala Sales Automation

**Last updated: October 31, 2026**

## Overview

EPR Gala Sales Automation is an administrative utility used to manage Google Apps Script projects and deployments associated with the Gala Sales Demo application.

## Google Account Access

The application uses Google OAuth authorization to access Google services on behalf of an authorized administrator.

Depending on the permissions granted, access may include Google Apps Script projects and deployments, Google Drive file information, and Google Cloud configuration.

## How Information Is Used

Google account authorization is used to authenticate the administrator and perform authorized project-management and deployment operations.

The application is not intended for public account registration, advertising, or marketing.

## Data Storage and Security

OAuth credentials are stored locally on an administrator-controlled computer and as encrypted secrets in GitHub Actions for automated deployment.

Access to these credentials is restricted to authorized administrators and deployment workflows.

## Information Sharing

The application is not designed to sell Google account information or share it with advertisers. Google and GitHub process information as necessary to provide their respective authentication, hosting, and deployment services.

## Data Retention and Revocation

Authorization credentials may be retained while automated deployment is in use. An administrator may revoke the application's Google account access through Google Account security settings and remove associated GitHub Actions secrets.
